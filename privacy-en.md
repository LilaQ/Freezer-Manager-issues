# Frostkeep Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Frostkeep helps you manage food in your freezer. For privacy questions or support, contact **info@emudev.de**.

## Inventory and photos

The app stores the item names, quantities, categories, locations, notes, dates and optional photos you provide on your device. Each item receives a random identifier. When iCloud is available, Apple's CloudKit syncs this data in your private iCloud database between devices using the same Apple Account. Apple's privacy terms also apply. The developer does not operate an inventory server or receive access to your items or photos through the app.

The app remains usable offline. iCloud syncing may be delayed and requires an available iCloud account and sufficient iCloud storage.

## Camera, photo picker and labels

With your permission, the camera reads QR labels or takes item photos. QR recognition happens on your device. Camera video is not streamed to the developer. A photo you explicitly capture becomes part of your inventory and may sync through iCloud.

The system photo picker lets you share individual selected images. The app does not request full photo library access. Photos are resized and re-encoded as JPEG files for storage.

QR codes contain only random item identifiers. Printed labels may also show an item's name, date and short code. A QR code does not grant other people access to your private iCloud database. Print data is sent to your selected printer through the system print dialog. Exported PDFs are saved where you choose and may contain the information printed on the labels.

## Purchases

Premium is offered as a one-time in-app purchase through Apple. The app uses StoreKit to load products and prices and to verify purchase status and restorations. Apple processes payments. The developer does not receive payment details in the app. Apple's terms govern its processing and retention of purchase information.

## No advertising or behavioral analytics

Frostkeep contains no advertising, tracking technologies or third-party analytics SDKs. You do not need to register an account with the developer. The operating system may process its own diagnostic information according to your Apple settings.

## Retention and deletion

Items and photos remain stored until you delete them. Taking an item out archives it rather than deleting it. You can permanently delete individual items in their details or delete the entire inventory, including photos, in Settings. Deletions sync to other devices when iCloud is available. Manage printed labels, exported PDFs and system backups separately. Uninstalling the app alone does not necessarily remove existing iCloud data.

## Your choices and contact

You can view, edit and delete your entries and change camera permissions in system settings. For questions or requests concerning your privacy rights, contact **info@emudev.de**. Please avoid sending sensitive inventory details or photos unless needed for your request. If you contact support by email, your message and sender information are used to handle your request.
