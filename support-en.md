# Frostkeep — Help & Support

Contact: **info@emudev.de**

## Managing items

Choose **Add item**, enter a name and optional quantity, location, dates, notes or photo, then save. The creation date is recorded automatically. In **Inventory**, search, filter by category and open item details.

**Take out of freezer** moves an item to **Taken out**. From there, put it back or delete it permanently. You can take items out with or without scanning. A use-by date is a personal reminder, not a food safety assessment.

## QR labels

In **Labels**, generate unique stickers before filling your freezer. Scan a new sticker to assign an item. An assigned code opens its item. For existing items, you can also enter the eight-character short code. If a short code is ambiguous, scan the full QR code.

Print the same code again from item details. Do not use copies of one code for different items. Scanning alone does not mark an item as taken out.

## Printing

Choose a preset or enter page and label dimensions, margins and gaps in millimeters. Skip starting positions to use a partially used sheet. Check the PDF preview and test alignment on plain paper first.

iPhone printing uses AirPrint; Mac printing uses the system print dialog. Your printer must support the selected paper or roll size. Choose the correct paper size and 100% or actual-size printing where available. If your printer is incompatible, save the PDF and print it with suitable printer software.

## iCloud

Use the same Apple Account on both devices and enable iCloud for Frostkeep. Syncing is automatic and changes may take time to appear. Check your connection, iCloud status and available iCloud storage if syncing is delayed. Local items remain available offline. Sharing a household inventory across different Apple Accounts is not supported.

## Premium

Five simultaneously active items are free. Taking items out frees up spaces. Premium unlocks unlimited active items with a one-time purchase and no subscription. Scanning, photos and label printing are also included for free. The current price is shown before purchase.

Use your purchase on supported devices with the same Apple Account. Choose **Restore purchases** if Premium is missing. Existing items remain available to view, edit and take out even without an active unlock.

## Permissions and deleting data

Allow camera access in system settings to scan or take photos. Share individual library photos through the system picker. Delete items individually or choose **Settings → Delete all items and photos**. Deletions sync through iCloud when available.

## Contacting support

Email **info@emudev.de** with a description of the problem, your app version and operating system. Do not send payment details, passwords or unnecessary personal information.

## Categories, locations and personal codes

Use **Organize** to name, assign symbols to, or remove categories and locations. They sync through iCloud. Existing locations are suggested when adding items, and inventory can be filtered by location. **Notes & label code** accepts an additional personal item code. Editing an item preserves its permanent QR identity.

## NFC and Bluetooth printers

On compatible iPhones, use **Scan → NFC** to read Frostkeep tags or prepare blank, writable NDEF tags. NTAG213 has enough space for an item identity. Choose freezer-safe labels. Item details can write an existing identity to a blank tag. Mac and iPad have no built-in NFC reader; items sync through iCloud.

Bluetooth-only printers may require their manufacturer’s app. Universal Bluetooth compatibility is not guaranteed. Use a compatible system printer or save the PDF for a suitable printing app. AirPrint chooses the closest supported paper size; check dimensions in the print dialog.

**Settings → iCloud sync → Refresh** checks connectivity and displays the check time. It does not force an immediate upload.
